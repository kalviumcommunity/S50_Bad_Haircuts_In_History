# Bad Haircuts In History

Welcome to the "List of Hilariously Bad Haircuts in History" repository, where we celebrate iconic hairstyle mishaps from the past. From mullets gone wild to eccentric beehives, this collection captures the most laughably disastrous haircuts that history has witnessed.

## Features

- **Share Your Opinions:** Users can share their thoughts, stories, or additional examples of hilariously bad haircuts in history.

- **Rate the Haircuts:** Express your sense of humor by rating the haircuts based on how funny they are. Your votes will contribute to the overall ranking of each disastrous hairstyle.

- **Dark Theme:** Switch to a dark theme for a more comfortable browsing experience, especially when diving into the world of historically bad haircuts.

## How to Contribute

1. **Share a Bad Haircut:**
   - Fork the repository.
   - Add a new entry to the list in the designated format (provide an image, description, and any relevant historical context).
   - Submit a pull request.

2. **Rate Existing Haircuts:**
   - Express your humor by rating existing haircuts. Simply comment on the haircut entry with your rating and reasoning.

3. **Feedback and Suggestions:**
   - Share your ideas, feedback, or suggestions by opening an issue. We appreciate your input in making this collection even more entertaining.
